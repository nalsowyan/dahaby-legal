Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Dahaby app for iPhone and Android. Version app-2026-11-26, effective 2026-11-26.

1. Who we are

Dahaby is made by Nasser AlSowyan (individual, no commercial registration), Unaizah, Al-Qassim, Saudi Arabia. Questions about privacy: na.alsowyan@gmail.com.

2. In short

Dahaby has no accounts and no server of ours. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

3. What stays on your phone

These are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, where we cannot see or access them:

  • Items in My Collection, and photos you choose to save with an item.
  • Zakat calculations, the option you follow, and hawl reminders.
  • Price alerts you set.
  • Settings: language, theme, text size, currency, weight unit, city and default karat.
  • Recent gold prices, so the app works offline and can draw the price chart.

4. What leaves your phone

  • Gold prices: the app requests the current gold price from Gold API (gold-api.com). The request contains no personal data. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address and basic technical details to that service. See its privacy policy: https://gold-api.com/privacy.
  • Notifications for price alerts and zakat reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; nothing is sent to us.
  • If you use Export or Share, the file or text goes only where you choose to send it.

5. Permissions

  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or pick a photo of a piece. Photos in the estimator are temporary and are not saved.
  • Notifications: only if you set a price alert or a zakat reminder.
  • Dahaby does not use your location, contacts or any other personal information on your phone.

6. Sensitive information

Zakat calculations can reveal religious belief, which Saudi law treats as sensitive. They never leave your phone, and the scholar option you choose is kept only while you use the calculator.

7. No tracking

Dahaby has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.

8. Legal basis and retention

We do not process personal data, so no legal basis applies and we keep nothing. Data on your phone stays there until you erase it or delete the app.

9. Security

Your data stays in the app’s private storage on your phone, protected by your phone’s passcode and encryption. The gold-price request is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

10. Transfers outside Saudi Arabia

We do not transfer personal data inside or outside the Kingdom. Your phone connects directly to Gold API to fetch the price; its servers may be outside Saudi Arabia, and the request carries no personal data other than your IP address.

11. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to be informed, to access, correct and destroy your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold none of your data, you exercise these rights directly on your phone: Settings → “Erase this device’s data” removes everything, and so does deleting the app.

Questions or requests: na.alsowyan@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also file a complaint with the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA, sdaia.gov.sa).

12. Children

Dahaby is a general-audience app and collects no data from anyone, including children.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its version date. Important changes will be shown in the app.

14. Language

This policy is available in Arabic and English. If they differ, the Arabic text prevails.

  • إشعارات تنبيهات الأسعار وتذكيرات الزكاة يجدولها جهازك بنفسه، ولا يُرسل إلينا شيء.
  • عند استخدام التصدير أو المشاركة، يذهب الملف أو النص إلى الجهة التي تختارها أنت فقط.

5. الأذونات
